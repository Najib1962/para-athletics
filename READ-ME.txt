THE RULE
--------
Youth is UNDER 20. As is normal in athletics it goes by the YEAR of
birth, not the exact date - so through the whole of 2026 that means
born 2007 or later, whatever month the birthday falls in.

    born 2007-12-31   Youth
    born 2006-01-01   Open

Enter a date of birth once and you never tick a box again. In January
the boundary moves on by itself.


WHERE TO ENTER IT
-----------------
Log in and open:

    https://para-athletics.onrender.com/age-groups.html

Every athlete now has a date box. Fill it in and the badge changes at
once, with a line underneath saying the category came from the date.

An athlete with no date on record keeps the Youth/Open button, so you
can still set them by hand - nothing you have already set is lost.


ON THE ENTRIES PAGE
-------------------
The Open/Youth box now shows the answer rather than asking for it: when
the athlete has a date of birth it is filled in and greyed out, because
there is nothing to decide. Athletes without a date still get the
choice, exactly as before.

Each entry shows "born 2007-12-31" beside the badge, or a "change" link
where no date is on record.


ON THE PRINTED START LISTS
--------------------------
Your sheet heads that column "الفئة العمرية" in Arabic and "D.O.B" in
English, so it now carries both - the date on the top line and Youth or
Open beneath it.
