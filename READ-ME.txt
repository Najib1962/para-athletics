WHY THIS MATTERS
----------------
World Para Athletics publishes TWO sets of point scores - open and
youth - and the same mark scores very differently under each.

A men's 100 m T37 in 13.20 seconds:

    Open    430 points
    Youth   998 points

Your app has always scored everything as open. If you run youth
competitions, every point has been wrong, and far too low.

The youth tables were already inside your app. There was simply no way
to switch an event over. Now there is.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Log in, then open:

    https://para-athletics.onrender.com/youth.html

Every event is listed with a badge saying OPEN or YOUTH, and a button
to switch it. There are also two buttons at the top to set the whole
competition at once.

Points recalculate immediately. No marks need re-entering.

The page sits behind the login, like your other admin pages.
