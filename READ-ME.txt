WHAT IT IS
----------
Blank sheets for the referees, in the layout of the ones you sent -
Arabic and English headings, one design for track and another for field.

The names, bib numbers, clubs, age categories and classes are filled in
from the entries already in the app. Everything the referee writes by
hand is left empty.

Log in, then open:

    https://para-athletics.onrender.com/print-startlists.html


THE TWO LAYOUTS
---------------
TRACK   No | Name | Bib | Club | Age/D.O.B | Class | Lane |
        1/100 | ss | mm | Time (s) | Points | Rank

FIELD   No | Name | Bib | Club | Age/D.O.B | Class | Weight |
        Att 1 to Att 6 | Best | Points | Rank

The right layout is chosen automatically from the discipline.

Above each table: Event, Event No, Class/Classes, Gender, Venue, Date,
Time - and Sector for field events, as on your sheet. At the foot there
are lines for the referee, the recorder and a signature.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Choose one event from the box, or leave it on "All events" to print the
whole competition at once. There is also a Sector box, since that is
written per session rather than stored with the event.

Press Print. It is set up as A4 landscape, one event per page. Choose
Save as PDF in the print box if you want a file.

Each sheet carries three spare blank lines for late entries - eight rows
minimum on a track sheet, twelve on a field sheet, matching yours.


A NOTE ON THE AGE COLUMN
------------------------
Your sheet heads that column "الفئة العمرية" (age category) in Arabic and
"D.O.B" in English. It shows the athlete's age category - Youth or Open -
which is what the app holds. If you later record dates of birth on
athletes, it will show those instead.


TESTED BEFORE SENDING
---------------------
