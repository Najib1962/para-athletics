WHAT IT DOES
------------
Each athlete is now marked Youth or Open. Their points come from the
matching World Para Athletics table.

Where one event contains both, they are ranked SEPARATELY - each group
with its own first, second and third, and its own gold, silver and
bronze.

They can run in the same race. Only the scoring is kept apart.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Log in, then open:

    https://para-athletics.onrender.com/age-groups.html

Every athlete is listed with a badge. Press the button to switch one,
or use the two buttons at the top to set everyone shown at once. There
is a search box, so you can set a whole club or class in one go.

Everyone counts as Open until you change them, so nothing shifts in a
competition that is all one age group.


WHY THE TWO GROUPS ARE RANKED APART
-----------------------------------
The two tables are not on the same scale. A men's 100 m T37:

    13.20 seconds as youth   998 points
    11.80 seconds as open    900 points

Ranked together, the slower athlete would beat the faster one by 98
points. That is not a close call - the tables measure against what is
outstanding FOR THAT AGE GROUP, so comparing across them is meaningless.
This is why World Para Athletics publishes them separately.


NAMES ARE MARKED ON RESULTS
---------------------------
In an event with both groups, names show as "Ahmed Khalil (Youth)" or
"Paul Wilson (Open)", so two athletes both ranked 1st are never
confused. In an event with only one group, nothing is added.


WHY THE MEDAL PAGES CHANGED
---------------------------
They used to take the first three rows of an event. With two rankings,
those three rows are all from one group, and the other group's
medallists were missed. They now go by the rank itself, which picks up
first, second and third in every group - and behaves exactly as before
when there is only one.
