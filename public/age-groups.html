👥 Age Categories

Enter a date of birth and the category looks after itself: under 20 is Youth, anyone older is Open. As is normal in athletics it goes by the year of birth, so through the whole of that means born or later.
Points then come from the matching World Para Athletics table. Where one event holds both, the two are ranked separately — each with its own first, second and third, and its own medals.
